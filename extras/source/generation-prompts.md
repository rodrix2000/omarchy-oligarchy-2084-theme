# Text-free scene generation prompts

All prompts used the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool and requested no
text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, deprecated visual motifs, or
recognizable people. The exact positive production direction is recorded here
for provenance and repeatability; retired concepts are documented only in the
asset audit.

## 01 — Privately Held

Premium retro-futurist corporate-utopia city at blue hour; inviting public
plaza, civic gardens, luminous transit lines, monumental administrative
arcology with a subtly inaccessible upper governance level; disciplined 1970s
World's Fair architecture translated through modern cinematic concept art;
deep indigo, civic navy, electric cyan, signal blue, restrained violet and
chrome; low-detail left third for tiled windows; architectural, elegant,
desirable before unsettling.

## 02 — Common Good

Bright optimistic civic-service pavilion in a clean future city; abundant
greenery, graceful public transit, anonymous distant silhouettes, luminous
service kiosk, elevated private administrative layer suggested through access
architecture; mid-century corporate futurism, premium civic technology,
daylight, cyan glass, cool white, soft blue and restrained violet; useful open
sky and calm left area.

## 03 — Public Access: Read Only

Minimal abstract public network sphere in a dark indigo space; many fine open
service orbits around a small polished split-diamond control core; vector grid,
subtle CRT texture, large negative-space region, architectural data
visualization rather than a literal UI; cyan, signal blue, violet, chrome,
almost-black navy.

## 04 — Enterprise Citizenship

Elegant futuristic civic enrollment lobby with three distinct access lanes and
glass service portals; open public lane, premium stakeholder lane, controlled
upper governance threshold; no guards or coercion; optimistic enterprise
service design, refined Art Deco geometry, clean reflective materials, civic
navy, cyan, violet, small amber accents; open upper-left wall for typography.

## 05 — Open Source / Closed Board

Futuristic repository operations center with luminous public code streams and
an elegant glass authorization core behind a separate ownership threshold;
developer-focused but not a literal readable UI; disciplined retro-futurist
control room, deep navy, cyan data light, blue, violet and restrained magenta;
low-detail left third and no people in focus.

## 06 — Preferred Future

Clean elevated monorail station in a desirable retro-future city; sweeping
canopy, sleek train, civic gardens, large blank institutional campaign display,
subtle private access bridge above public concourse; premium 1970s transit
advertising mood, cyan and blue evening light with sparse warm amber; open
display and calm left region.

## 07 — Annual General Election

Premium abstract shareholder-resolution display installed in a bright civic
atrium; three elegant blank decision panels, proportional ownership bars as
pure geometry, no recognizable ballot or government seal; polished corporate
futurism, cool navy, chrome, cyan, violet and restrained amber; sophisticated,
calm, useful negative space.

## 08 — Public Float: 0.00%

Minimal planetary city-network composition in deep indigo; elegant central
ownership core with broad public service orbits and distributed civic lights;
quiet vector-astronomical diagram blended with cinematic depth, extensive
negative space, cyan, blue, violet and chrome with one small amber control
point; no literal text or recognizable planet.
