# Composition notes

All generated scene bases are text-free. Exact copy is added in a separate,
deterministic raster-compositing pass using installed Noto Sans and JetBrains
Mono fonts. Each background keeps essential text outside the outer 5% margin
and reserves a low-detail area for tiled windows.

The final raster exports are sRGB JPEGs at 3840×2160. Textless masters use the
same dimensions. Dedicated ultrawide variants are 3440×1440 and are cropped or
recomposed from the clean scene rather than stretched.

The split governance diamond is original vector work. Its public ring and open
lower share surround a filled upper control share with a small permission-gate
notch. Thin orbit lines imply public infrastructure without turning the symbol
into a coin or ceremonial mark.

The editable emblem master uses a 1024×1024 view box. The distributed
`unlock.png` is an 800×480 transparent canvas with the emblem centered at 460
px high. This follows the current Quattro Plymouth behavior: the preview and
installer copy unlock art at native size without scaling, and a square 1024 px
canvas pushes the password field below the 1920×1080 preview. The shipped size
keeps the full emblem and authentication controls visible.
